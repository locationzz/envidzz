SimpleRTP

v1.4.2 · mc 1.21+

Random-teleport that respects the terrain.

  • /rtp opens a dimension-picker GUI: Overworld / Nether / End
  • Overworld lands only on open-to-sky surfaces — never caves or overhangs
  • Nether / End scan vertically for a solid block with 2 air above
  • Avoids lava, water, fire, magma, cactus, powder snow, cobwebs
  • Per-dimension radius, cooldown, and max attempts — all configurable
  • Admin: /rtpadmin enable|disable <dim>, status, reload (alias /rtpa)

TeamX

v2.1.0 · mc 1.21+

Teams with weight — roles, alliances, a shared chest.

  • Team panel GUI; team home with warmup (cancels on move/damage)
  • Shared ender chest persisted to disk across restarts
  • Roles: Owner / Officer / Member; team chat (/tc) and announce
  • Alliances (request/accept/break) — allies can't damage each other
  • Tab list shows team name + color; rename, recolor, disband
  • Admin: scan every team chest for an item; read-only snapshots

LiteBanX (BanX)

v3.0.1 · MIT · mc 1.21+

A punishment suite that scales to a full moderation platform.

  • ban, tempban, ipban, tempipban, mute, tempmute, warn, kick, note
  • /banx <sub> with full tab completion; short forms too
  • Order-insensitive parsing — duration and reason in any order
  • Offline players via DB cache + async Mojang lookup
  • Reason templates, warning points, and auto-escalation
  • Alt detection; themed screens; broadcast titles
  • MySQL (HikariCP) or SQLite with auto schema migration
  • PlaceholderAPI hooks; webhook; importers for AdvancedBan & LiteBans
